Changelog

All notable changes to Exam Study Planner are documented here.

[3.2] — 2026-08-09

Fixed

• Made the Mock Test entry modal independently scrollable on smaller
screens.
• Ensured the Save Mock action remains reachable on phones and
tablets.
• Improved mobile usability for long forms without changing the tested
V3.1 feature set.

Preserved

• Smart MIS dashboard
• Exam management
• Subject and topic management
• Subject-specific topic addition
• Three-stage revision tracking
• Pending-revision indicators
• Negative-marking calculation
• Mock-test analytics
• Daily study planning
• Local browser storage

[3.1] — 2026-08-09

Restored

• Subject-wise Revision 1, Revision 2 and Revision 3 checkboxes.
• Pending-revision indication for topics that are not yet completed.
• Configurable negative marking in mock tests.
• Automatic unattempted-question calculation.
• Marks after negative marking.
• Subject-specific Add Topic workflow so topics can be added directly
inside their subject without repeatedly selecting the subject.

Improved

• Revision analytics now reflect actual revision checkbox progress.
• Mobile interaction and workflow improvements.

[3.0] — 2026-08-08

Added

• Smart MIS dashboard.
• Dashboard preparation-progress visualization.
• Exam, Subjects, Daily Plan, Revision, Mock Tests and MIS & Analytics
sections.
• KPI cards and preparation insights.
• Quick actions for common study-management tasks.
• Subject performance and mock-test analytics.

[2.8]

Added

• Earlier study-planning and tracking improvements that formed the
functional baseline for the V3 redesign.

Development notes

Version numbers reflect the project’s iterative development and
practical testing. Features are added or changed based on actual use of
the study planner.
